Privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy covers Taxi Hustle, a game for iPhone and iPad made by Keabetswe Phokela, an individual developer ("we", "us").

In short: Taxi Hustle does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. No third-party services are built into the app.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings (such as your level, coins, vehicles, daily rewards, your saved trip, and sound and comfort settings) are kept only on your device. We can't see them. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Apple

If you choose to share crash reports or diagnostics with developers in your device settings, or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. We use any feedback you send us only to reply and to improve the game.

Children

Taxi Hustle is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the contact details on the support page.
