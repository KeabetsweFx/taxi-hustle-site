Support

Taxi Hustle is a single-player mental-maths and driving game for iPhone and iPad. At every pickup, add up each group's fares, board the group that pays best and still fits in your seats, then swipe through traffic to the next pickup. It's free, with no ads and no in-app purchases, and it works offline. It's available first in South Africa.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. There's no sign-in, and the game plays fully offline.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. It isn't backed up to a server, so it doesn't move to a new device.

How do I start again from the beginning?

Delete the app and install it again. This erases all progress and settings on that device.

Is the money in the game real?

No. The coins (shown as "R", like the rand) are earned only by playing. There's nothing to buy and no real money involved.

The game is too fast, or runs slowly on my device.

Open Settings in the game. There are comfort options for a relaxed timer, reduced motion and lighter 3D for older devices.

What data does the app collect?

None. See the privacy policy.
