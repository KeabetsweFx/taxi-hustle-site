Taxi Hustle

Count fast. Load smart. Make money.

Taxi Hustle puts you behind the wheel of a South African minibus taxi. Add up each group's fares in your head, board the group that pays best and still fits, then drive to the next pickup.

A free game for iPhone and iPad. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts.
